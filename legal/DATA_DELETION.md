# Delete DoseTracker Data

DoseTracker is provided by VeriCode. This page explains local erasure and how to request deletion of information received by VeriCode. Updated October 23, 2026.

## Delete records on your device

Open DoseTracker → Settings → Erase all data, then confirm. This removes local profiles, medicines, schedules, dose history, stored photos, preferences and the app-lock flag; it also cancels reminders. Retry if the app reports a failure or erasure was interrupted.

VeriCode cannot remotely access or erase your local health records. Archiving a profile keeps its records; it is not erasure. Preferences exports contain no profiles, medicine records or dose history. Copies already shared outside the app remain under the destination's control.

## Request deletion of support emails

The app does not send health records or technical reports to VeriCode. The only information VeriCode receives is email you choose to send. To have it deleted, email support@vericodestudio.com with the subject “DoseTracker data deletion request” and identify the email address used. Do not send medicine names, prescriptions, health records, passwords or identity documents.

VeriCode uses this information to locate and address the request. Limited verification may be needed. We will explain any information we must retain and the reason.

## What is retained

Local records remain on your device until deleted, erased or removed with the app.

VeriCode deletes support and deletion-request emails within 90 days after the request is resolved, unless legally required to retain them longer. Unresolved requests are retained while needed to handle them. There is no online DoseTracker account to delete.

## Privacy and contact

The Privacy Policy explains local records, permissions and service providers. Send questions to support@vericodestudio.com.
