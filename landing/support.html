Recommended Checklist

Check your reminder settings

Android includes battery-saving features that can occasionally pause notifications when your phone is stationary. These checks can help, but cannot guarantee on-time delivery. Keep a separate plan for time-critical medication:

1

Allow Exact Alarms

Open your phone's Settings → Apps → DoseTracker → Alarms & Reminders and make sure Allowed is turned on. This lets DoseTracker wake the screen on schedule.

2

Keep Notifications Enabled

Ensure notifications are toggled on in Settings → Apps → DoseTracker → Notifications. Check that notification volume is turned up so you can hear the chime.

3

Set Battery to Unrestricted

In Settings → Apps → DoseTracker → Battery, select Unrestricted. This prevents Android from putting the app to sleep during overnight hours.

💡
In-App Reminder Status: You can open Settings → Reminder status directly inside DoseTracker to see an instant diagnostic check of all permissions.

Common Questions & Solutions

⏰

Reminders & Schedules

Why didn't my reminder ring at the exact minute?

If an alarm is delayed by a few minutes, Android's battery optimizer may have placed the phone in deep sleep (Doze mode). Follow the 3-step checklist above to set battery usage to "Unrestricted" and enable exact alarm permissions.

How does the Snooze feature work?

In-app snooze uses your chosen duration (5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes). The notification action snoozes for 15 minutes. A snooze requests another local reminder without marking the dose taken; permissions and system restrictions still apply.

Can I set as-needed (PRN) medications?

Yes! When adding a medication, select "As Needed". These medications won't ring automatic alarms, but remain accessible in your Today dashboard so you can record doses whenever you take them.

Profiles & Family Care

How do I add a family member or pet?

Tap the profile avatar icon in the top header and tap Add Person. You can enter their name, choose an avatar color, add a photo, and specify whether they are a parent, child, or pet.

How will I know if someone else's dose is overdue?

The profile switcher at the top displays a red alert badge with the count of overdue doses. Tapping the switcher lets you jump directly into that person's schedule to confirm their medication.

Can I archive a profile after a treatment finishes?

Yes. Inside People & Profiles, select the person and tap Archive Profile. This pauses all their reminders while keeping their historical records safe on your device.

📦

Supply & Refill Alerts

How does DoseTracker know when I'm running low?

When you record a taken dose, the app subtracts it from your recorded stock. An in-app warning appears at or below your threshold. Update refills and counts yourself. These warnings do not send background refill notifications.

How do I update my supply after picking up a refill?

Go to the Medicines tab, tap on the medication, and tap Refill Stock. Enter the new number of pills to reset your inventory in seconds.

Privacy, Lock & Deletion

Can I lock the app with my fingerprint?

Yes. In Settings → App Lock, turn on biometric authentication. The app will require your fingerprint, face recognition, or device passcode every time it is opened.

How do I permanently delete all my information?

Go to Settings → Erase all data and confirm. This permanently purges all profile records, medicines, history, photos, and alarms from your phone.

Do you have access to my prescription list?

Your health records stay on your device. If you opt in under Settings → Optional diagnostics, the categories you choose—error reports, diagnostic logs and performance traces—go to Sentry for VeriCode. Each category is off by default and excludes health records. See our privacy policy for details.
