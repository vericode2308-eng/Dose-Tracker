Effortless Onboarding

Add Any Medication in 4 Straightforward Steps

No convoluted medical jargon or required cloud accounts. A clean sequential flow makes configuring dosages, complex recurrences, and stock alerts quick and foolproof.

Step 01

Details & Formulation

Enter brand name, strength (mg/mcg/ml), dosage form (tablet, capsule, drops), and custom color tag for fast recognition.

Step 02

Frequency & Timing

Choose daily, specific weekdays, or as-needed. Assign exact reminder timestamps matching your breakfast, lunch, or bedtime routine.

Track your supply count. Define a low-stock alert threshold so you're notified days before finishing your last blister pack.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about alarms, Android permissions, privacy guarantees, and data management.

DoseTracker schedules alarms directly with the Android operating system using the SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM permission and the device's native alarm manager. Android stores these triggers in hardware RTC registers, allowing the alarm to fire on time without any active internet connection or background network polling.

Yes! DoseTracker includes complete Multi-Profile management. You can create independent profiles for yourself, elderly parents, children, or pets. Each profile maintains its own medication catalog, schedules, stock counts, and history. The profile switcher shows notification badges if another profile has an overdue dose.

Android uses Doze mode to preserve battery when devices are motionless. By requesting exact-alarm access and configuring high-priority notification channels, DoseTracker instructs the system to allow alarm wake-ups during maintenance windows. Our Reminder Status settings screen guides you through granting exact alarm access and battery unmetered execution for peak dependability.

Not unless you want them to. In DoseTracker's Notification Privacy settings, you can select between Public, Private, or Secret modes. In Private or Secret mode, the reminder notification redacts the medication name and dosage, showing only a discreet message such as "Scheduled Dose Due".

Yes. Since DoseTracker stores everything in an app-private SQLite database (`dosetracker.db`), you have complete ownership. You can export your full dosage history, profiles, and medicine schedules as a standard JSON file directly through the Settings screen whenever you choose.
