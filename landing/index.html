Quick Setup

Add a Medicine Step by Step

A step-by-step assistant guides you through name, schedule, and stock warnings without confusing medical menus.

Step 1

Name & Strength

Type the medicine name, strength, form (tablets, drops, syrup), and pick a cheerful color tag.

Step 2

When to Take It

Track daily, weekday, interval, or as-needed schedules. Automatic reminders support ongoing daily or weekday schedules starting today; other patterns are tracking only.

Step 3

Supply & Refills

Enter your stock and warning threshold. Check in-app warnings and keep counts up to date.

Step 4

Review & Finish

Review and save. If you enabled reminders for a supported schedule, the app requests them through your device.

DoseTracker Privacy Policy

DoseTracker is operated by VeriCode. Health records stay on your device. Optional diagnostics are off by default; if you enable individual categories, the selected error reports, diagnostic logs and performance traces are sent to Sentry. Reports already sent are not removed by erasing local data.
