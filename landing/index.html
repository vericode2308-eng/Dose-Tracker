Built from Code Reality: DoseTracker stores 100% of your records on your phone. We do not operate remote user accounts, databases, or ad tracking.

1. Overview and Core Philosophy

DoseTracker is an offline-first medication management application. We built DoseTracker on a simple premise: your medical regimens, prescription history, and personal health data belong entirely to you, not on remote cloud servers.

  • Zero Remote Accounts: You do not need to register, provide an email address, enter a phone number, or create a password to use the app.
  • No Remote Databases: We do not operate a cloud backend for your health data. All profile, medication, schedule, and dose adherence data is stored strictly on your local device.
  • No Ad Networks or Tracking: The app contains no advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party behavioral analytics services.

2. Information You Enter

All information is provided directly by you:

  • Profiles: Profile names, relationship notes, and optional profile photos selected from your phone.
  • Medications: Medicine names, doses, instructions, refill counts, and scheduled reminder times.
  • History: Timestamps when doses are recorded or snoozed.

3. Storage & Security

Everything is saved within your phone's private application storage. Android automated cloud backup is disabled to prevent accidental cloud uploads. You can also activate optional fingerprint or face recognition lock to safeguard screen access.

4. Erase & Delete at Any Time

You can tap "Erase all data" inside Settings at any time to permanently remove all profiles, medications, doses, photos, and alarms from your device. Uninstalling the app also purges all records immediately.

1. Acceptance & Purpose

DoseTracker is a free personal tool to help you organize your daily medications and care for family members. You do not need an account or subscription to use it.

2. Medical Disclaimer

You provide the medication names, doses, and schedules. DoseTracker does not verify prescriptions, check interactions, diagnose illnesses, or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. For medical emergencies, always contact your local emergency service immediately.

3. Reminders & Reliability

Reminders depend on your device's battery settings and notifications permissions. When critical medication timing is required, always maintain a secondary dependable backup.
